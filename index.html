Top three activities to do at Aizawl

Shop and Experience Local Life at Bara Bazaar

It offers a vibrant collection of local goods, street food, and trendy thrift fashion.

Discover Heritage at the Mizoram State Museum

Located on MacDonald Hill, this ethnographic museum features historic relics, traditional costumes, and ancient artifacts.

Enjoy Views at Durtlang Hills and Viewpoints

Durtlang Hills provide sweeping vistas of Aizawl and the surrounding valleys.
